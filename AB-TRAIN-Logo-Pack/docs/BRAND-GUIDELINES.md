# AB TRAIN — Brand usage guidelines

## Canonical identity

The user-provided `production/mark/ab-monogram-source-unaltered.png` is the **only approved master mark**. It is preserved byte-for-byte. All production variants are derivative **raster** exports or composed lockups. There is **no claim that these are edited vector logos**.

## Professional usage requirements

1. Do not stretch or crop into the AB letters. Leave clear space around the mark (recommended >= 20% of its displayed height).
2. Apply theme-specific variants and verify actual contrast against the composited background.
3. Use purple for primary actions and focus states; decorative use should not drown out the booking action.
4. Zellige should be subtle, typically in a single separator/footer region, decorative (`aria-hidden`) and absent from print.
5. Do not add glass blur behind fields where it harms readability or performance. Provide non-blur fallback.
6. Keep a visible `SAMPLE — NOT VALID FOR TRAVEL` disclaimer on mock tickets; don't claim official booking authority.
7. Preserve accessible images: informative logo accessible name `AB TRAIN`; ornamental images empty `alt` and `aria-hidden` where appropriate.

## Quality limitation

The supplied raster monogram contains slight roughness/noise from the source imagery. These exports preserve the visual mark, but do not constitute a handcrafted and approved vector master. For a trademark-grade identity, produce a manual redraw separately and seek owner's visual sign-off before replacing the master.
